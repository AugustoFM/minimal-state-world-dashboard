# C7 - External block v2 (commodity prices, world rates, risk premium, commodity revenue)

Owner: Codex (estimation, back-test), Claude (data, review, model integration). Requested by the user 2026-10-21
after D2 showed C6 explains ~0.8 pp of the 24% 2006-2010 boom and nothing of 2014-2016.

## Why
Literature review (2026-10-21, in the session log): commodity prices and world financial conditions move
emerging-market output through (A) income/demand, (B) country spreads and domestic interest rates
(Uribe-Yue 2006; Fernández-González-Rodríguez 2018; Drechsel-Tenreyro 2018), (C) investment
(Céspedes-Velasco 2012: a 60% commodity boom ~ +1% output, +3.5 pp investment rate) and
(D) commodity-linked public revenue (Frankel-Végh-Vuletin 2013; IFI RAF 111). C6 has only a
terms-of-trade-to-GDP coefficient and FX pass-through.

## Protocol (fixed before estimation)
- Primary specifications are fixed in the Codex prompt; alternatives are robustness only.
- Fit window 2000-2014; test window 2015-2024 (2010-2017 / 2018-2024 for revenue, data from 2010).
  The test window is never used to choose a specification or parameter.
- Report: coefficients with HAC standard errors, out-of-sample RMSE/mean error against the current C6
  and a naive benchmark, and the literature benchmarks. A channel whose out-of-sample error is not
  better than naive is reported as not supported and is not wired into the model.

## Data
data/brazil/external_observed.csv (scripts/fetch_brazil_external.py), data/brazil/backtest_observed.csv,
data/raw/brazil/demonstrativos-governo-geral.xlsx (dividends 1412, concessions 1415, % GDP, 2010+).

## Out of scope
Domestic shocks (2014-16 politics, Lava Jato, monetary tightening) stay in the residual; they are
represented in the Monte Carlo by bootstrapping back-test residuals (later task), not by an equation.
