# C5: Long-run feedback block

- Owner: Codex builds, Claude reviews (parameters sourced by Claude in `config/parameters.yaml` -> `long_run_feedback`)
- Depends on: C2, C4 (C3 for the inequality channel)
- Output: `src/feedback.py`

## Why
C2 captures short-run demand effects (multipliers) and revenue moving with GDP, but potential growth follows its historical trend in every scenario. Spending choices also change growth and financing costs over 5-20 years:

1. Schooling -> human capital -> potential output (PWT human-capital index).
2. Health (life expectancy) -> labour productivity.
3. Public investment -> public capital stock -> potential output.
4. Debt -> sovereign risk premium -> interest rate -> debt dynamics.
5. Net inequality -> growth (switched off until C3 exists).

Channels 1-3 favour keeping spending, channel 4 favours lower debt, channel 5 can cut either way. All are held to the same evidence standard.

## What to do
Run every scenario twice, `without_feedback` (current C2) and `with_feedback`, and report each channel's contribution by switching it off one at a time. Use only parameters from `config/parameters.yaml` (`long_run_feedback`, `long_run_feedback_choices`). Model cohort replacement so that schooling gains of school-age cohorts reach adult mean years of schooling. Download and archive an initial public capital stock for Brazil (e.g. IMF Investment and Capital Stock Dataset) before using channel 3.
