# C6: External block (terms of trade, exchange rate)

- Owner: Codex builds, Claude reviews (parameters sourced by Claude in `config/parameters.yaml` -> `external_block`)
- Depends on: C2 (and C5 for the risk-premium link)
- Output: `src/external.py`

## Why
The 2003-2010 review (data in `data/brazil/backtest_observed.csv`) showed that exports, reserves, FDI, the exchange rate and falling interest rates moved with a large terms-of-trade boom while the primary surplus stayed at 3.2-3.7% of GDP. A closed-economy fiscal model cannot separate policy from external luck, so it would over- or under-credit policy.

## What to do
1. Treat the external environment (commodity terms of trade, BRL/USD) as an exogenous, uncertain input: block-bootstrap whole historical paths of observed annual changes; also report fixed boom / neutral / bust reference paths.
2. Map terms of trade to GDP with `external_block.gdp_wrt_commodity_prices`, split into transitory and potential parts per `potential_vs_actual_output`; revenue follows GDP as in C2.
3. Map the exchange rate to inflation with `exchange_rate_pass_through` (deflator path used by C2 debt dynamics).
4. Apply the same external path to every policy scenario, so differences between scenarios stay attributable to policy.
5. Source a longer terms-of-trade series (FUNCEX/IPEAData) and the FX-linked share of public debt (BCB) before using them; record them in `data/raw/SOURCES.md`.
