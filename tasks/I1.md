# I1 - Interest rates: where Brazil is now, what moves them, and an evidence-based S0 rate path

Owner: Codex (data build, estimation, report), Claude (data download, literature and programme review in
`docs/interest_rates_review.md`, review, model wiring). Requested by the user 2026-10-21: "analyse well the interest rate,
they are now at a high peak in Brazil ... document well why the models vary".

## Why
S0 holds the real implicit interest rate on general-government debt at 5.05% (2021-2025 median) for 20 years. The
interest-growth term is 71% of S0's debt increase (D3, `docs/debt_models_review.md`). The Selic is 13.75% (Oct 2026; peak
15%), 12-month IPCA 4.58%: the ex-post real policy rate is ~8.8%, against a BCB neutral-rate estimate reported at ~5%
(press; market views 6-8%). Brazil's 2000-2024 r-g (+2.83 pp) is the highest of 26 emerging economies (D3). The model
needs (a) a defensible real-rate path from today's peak, (b) the pass-through from the Selic to the implicit rate on debt,
(c) evidence on how expected fiscal paths move expected rates, a channel identical for every scenario.

## Protocol (fixed before looking at results)
Data (read-only, `data/raw/brazil/rates/`, see `data/raw/SOURCES.md`): BCB SGS 432 (Selic target, daily), 4390 (Selic,
% per month), 4189 (Selic annualised, monthly), 433/13522 (IPCA monthly/12m), 13762 (DBGG % GDP), 4513 (DLSP % GDP); Focus
annual expectations (Selic, IPCA, PIB Total, Resultado primário, Dívida bruta do governo geral from 2018, Dívida líquida do
setor público), JSON from the BCB Olinda API (fields Indicador, IndicadorDetalhe, Data, DataReferencia, Mediana, ...;
text may be in a non-UTF-8 encoding: handle it). Other existing inputs: `data/raw/brazil/external/fred_DGS10.csv` (US 10y),
`data/raw/brazil/external/ipeadata_JPM366_EMBI366.json` (EMBI+ Brazil, to mid-2024), and the general-government interest
payable / debt history used by `src/macro.py` (`read_general_government_fiscal_workbook`, `read_bcb_debt_history`; read,
do not modify). Use the end-of-month value of each Focus series (last survey date in the month, median).

1. Rates history, monthly 2000-2026: Selic (monthly average of the target), 12m IPCA, ex-post real Selic (Fisher);
   ex-ante one-year real rate = Focus Selic for next calendar year minus Focus IPCA for next calendar year (Fisher).
   Report today's level and its percentile in the 2000-2026 and 2010-2026 history.
2. Medium-term expected real rate (primary market measure): for each month, Focus Selic minus Focus IPCA (Fisher) for
   the calendar year t+3 (three years ahead). Robustness: t+2 and the longest horizon available.
3. Fiscal-expectations association (primary specification): monthly first differences, 2010-01 to 2026-09,
   d(medium-term expected real rate) = a + b1 d(Focus expected primary balance, year t+2, % GDP) + b2 d(Focus expected
   public debt, year t+3: DLSP; DBGG robustness from 2018) + b3 d(US 10y) + e, Newey-West SE (6 lags). Report b1 and b2
   in basis points per pp of GDP. Robustness: levels with a time trend; 2017-2026; adding d(EMBI) to mid-2024. These are
   market associations, not causal effects; say so.
4. Event table (descriptive): change in the medium-term expected real rate, expected primary balance and expected debt
   from the month before to two months after: EC 95 spending cap (Dec 2016), pension reform final approval (Oct 2019),
   PEC da Transição (Dec 2022), LC 200 approval (Aug 2023), fiscal package (Nov-Dec 2024), 2026 first round (Oct 2026,
   partial: last observation).
5. Pass-through to the debt: annual implicit nominal rate on general-government debt (interest payable / average debt)
   regressed on the annual average Selic of the same and previous year, 2010-2025 (n small: say so). Report the long-run
   pass-through and the share of the Selic change passed through in year 1.
6. Proposed S0 real-rate path, 2026-2045, for the lead developer (proposal only; do not change model files):
   (a) 2026-2029: expected real policy rate from the latest Focus Selic and IPCA medians per year; (b) after the Focus
   horizon, linear convergence over 5 years to a long-run real policy rate = BCB neutral (5.0%, as configurable input
   with range 4.5-6.0 from the review); (c) implicit real rate on debt = pass-through of (a)-(b) using item 5, anchored
   to the 2025 implicit real rate; (d) a fiscal term = b2 x (scenario expected debt minus S0 2025 debt), applied to every
   scenario identically, with b2 from item 3 (and its confidence interval). Compare the resulting S0 path with today's
   fixed 5.05%. Write it to `config/i1_proposed.yaml` with derivations.
