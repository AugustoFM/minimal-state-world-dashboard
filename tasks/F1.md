# F1: Fiscal delivery ratio (targets vs outturns, 1999-2025)

- Owner: Claude (sources and definitions), Codex (assembly script and tests once the source files are archived)
- Depends on: -
- Output: `data/brazil/fiscal_delivery.csv`, `config/parameters.yaml` -> `fiscal_delivery`, used by P1

## Why
Election programmes announce fiscal plans; governments deliver part of them. Instead of using a government's growth record as a forecast (which would mix policy with global conditions and starting points), F1 measures something attributable to fiscal management: how far each year's primary-balance outturn landed from the target the same government set in the LDO.

## What to do
1. For every year 1999-2025, from that year's LDO "Anexo de Metas Fiscais" (Planalto/Câmara/Senado) record:
   - the original primary-balance target (consolidated public sector and central government, in R$ and % of GDP as published);
   - every legal revision during the year (law number, date) and the final revised target;
   - permitted deductions (e.g. PPI/PAC investment abatements, state-owned enterprise exclusions) and the tolerance band after LC 200/2023.
2. Outturn from BCB (consolidated public sector, SGS 5793, "abaixo da linha") and Tesouro RTN (central government), same concept and units as the target.
3. Per year: miss vs original target and vs final target (pp of GDP and as a ratio of delivered to promised adjustment); flag years with target changes, deductions used, or extraordinary events (1999 devaluation, 2008-09 crisis, 2020 pandemic).
4. Summarise by government period (FHC II, Lula I-II, Dilma, Temer, Bolsonaro, Lula III) and pooled, with the number of years and the spread.

## How the model uses it (P1)
- Default: the pooled distribution of misses is applied identically to both 2026 programmes: each programme's promised fiscal path is delivered with a sampled shortfall.
- Sensitivity only: each party's own record. Flávio Bolsonaro has no presidential record; Jair Bolsonaro's 2019-22 period is a weak proxy and must be labelled as such. 2020 (pandemic, targets suspended) is excluded or reported separately.

## Rules
- Never invent, interpolate or fill missing values without a source; keep value + year + concept (consolidated vs central; R$ vs % GDP).
- Record every source (URL, date, licence) in `data/raw/SOURCES.md`.
- Report the same statistics for every government; no ranking language in outputs.
