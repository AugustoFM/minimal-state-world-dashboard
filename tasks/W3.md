# W3 - Anti-corruption sensitivity, switchable per programme (user request 2026-10-22)

Owner: Codex (implementation, tests), Claude (evidence, mapping, review, runs). Off by default. Identical parameters
whichever programme it is switched on for. No candidate is assumed more or less corrupt (perception indices do not
rank recent governments: `docs/governance.md`). The sensitivity asks: "what if this programme's stated
anti-corruption measures deliver effects of the size the evidence supports?"

## Channels (each reported separately; parameters drawn per draw on a new stream `[seed, 8]`, same draw for every scenario)
1. **Leakage reduction in social-service spending** (audits, transparency, integrity plans). The model's leakage share
   (2.8%-13%, `config/parameters.yaml` governance) is cut by r, r ~ triangular(0.08, 0.25, 0.50): low = Avis, Ferraz &
   Finan (JPE 2018: past random CGU audit -> 8% less future corruption, 1,949 municipalities); mode and high are
   choices (high = existing "leakage halved" sensitivity). Effect: same spending -> more effective services (C4 health
   and mortality, C5 schooling/health channels via effective services); no fiscal saving.
2. **Procurement savings** (e-procurement, price databases, integrity). Saving s of central-government purchases of
   goods and services (Tesouro COFOG GFS "USO DE BENS E SERVICOS", from `data/raw/brazil/education/
   tesouro_cofog_gc_2010_2024_aggregated.csv.gz`, share of GDP in 2024), s ~ triangular(0, 0.01, 0.03), ramped over
   2027-2030. Effect: primary-balance improvement (non-tax, no multiplier on households, like PZ lost dividends but
   positive; use the same `non_tax_revenue_by_year` path or a spending reduction in "other_current" without service
   loss - choose one and document). Evidence weak (federal 2007 report: 12.7% price saving in electronic auctions vs
   reference price; case studies): wide range, labelled.
3. **INSS fraud protection** (deduction/consignado fraud). Note: the 2019-2024 associative-deduction fraud took money
   from retirees, not from the Treasury (CGU/PF estimate R$ 6.3 bn over 2019-2024; CPMI majority report R$ 3.3 bn;
   associative deductions ended by law in Nov 2025). Effect: protects pensioners' income in C3 (pension income of
   65+ recipients raised by f vs S0, f = 0 to R$ 1.0 bn/yr spread over pension recipients, central R$ 0.5 bn), no
   fiscal effect. Reported as poverty-among-65+ change.
4. **Lower major-scandal probability** (institutions, SOE governance). G2 annual probability (3%-8%) multiplied by
   (1 - q), q ~ triangular(0, 0.25, 0.50) (judgment; labelled). Effect: fewer GDP-loss events in the scenario. NOTE:
   G2 currently shares one event path across scenarios; implement by thinning the shared event sequence for the
   scenario that has the channel on (each event kept with probability 1 - q using a per-event uniform drawn once per
   draw), so draws stay paired.
Optional, off: long-run CPI improvement (G2 config, placeholder to verify) - not part of W3 runs.

## Programme mapping (`config/w3_anticorruption.yaml`, lead mapping from the TSE texts; quotes and pages)
- Lula (PT programme pp. 15-18): deepen the 2025-2027 Integrity and Anti-Corruption Plan (prevention, investigation,
  accountability); AI-based Transparency Portal; stronger Ouvidoria-Geral; debate on parliamentary amendments and
  the secret budget -> channels 1 and 4.
- Flávio (PL programme pp. 12, 69-72): transparency/traceability of parliamentary amendments; spending evaluation and
  beneficiary identification; strengthen the 2016 State-Owned Companies Law, market recruitment of SOE executives,
  shield SOE pension funds; INSS anti-fraud package and payroll-loan governance -> channels 1, 3 and 4.
- Channel 2 (procurement): neither programme quantifies it; available as a symmetric add-on to both.
CLI `--anti-corruption {none,lula,flavio,both}` (default none), `--anti-corruption-mapping {programme,full}`
(programme = channels mapped above; full = channels 1-4 for whichever is on), recorded in metadata with the mapping.

## Outputs
New indicators: `anticorruption_leakage_contribution_*` (services/health), `anticorruption_fiscal_saving_pp_gdp`,
`anticorruption_scandal_gdp_contribution_pct`, `anticorruption_pensioner_income_change_pct`; existing indicators change
only for the programme(s) switched on. `docs/w3_anticorruption.md` (evidence table, mapping with quotes/pages,
limitations: qualitative commitments mapped by judgment; sizes partly assumptions).

## Tests
Off = bit-identical; on for one programme changes only that programme's rows; channel signs; paired event thinning
keeps draws aligned; symmetry of parameters between programmes under `both`.
