## Annual flow arithmetic

Interest saved equals sale value times the rate; dividends lost equal the 2015-2025 cycle measure available for the selected stakes. This deterministic comparison applies no block discount or completion haircut.

| package | fixed-rate interest saved (R$ bn/yr) | dividends lost (R$ bn/yr) | break-even dividend yield | I1 2027-30 interest saved range (R$ bn/yr) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| f5 | 0.742 | 0.000 | 0.000% | 1.050-1.134 |
| broad | 1.264 | 0.179 | 0.716% | 1.787-1.931 |
| broad_bb_petrobras | 19.689 | 25.047 | 6.424% | 27.843-30.083 |

At the fixed S0 real rate, the break-even yield is 5.05% before transaction discounts: above it, foregone dividends exceed first-year interest savings; below it, interest savings exceed dividends. The I1 comparison uses its 2027-2030 real debt-rate path (7.14%-7.72%).

Asset-sale receipts reduce gross debt. They do not create an equal improvement in public-sector net worth: at a fair price the state exchanges an equity asset for lower liabilities. The recurring fiscal flow is therefore shown separately as interest saved less dividends lost.

## Petrobras and oil

ANP monthly data average 1,499 thousand barrels/day in 2002, 3,850 in 2025, and 4,412 through August 2026. Real direct-Union Petrobras receipts averaged R$0.916bn a year in the 2015-2020 low regime and R$33.666bn in the 2021-2025 high regime (2025 reais). For the full 36.61% state stake, the corresponding lost-dividend measures are R$1.169bn and R$42.990bn, scaling the direct receipts by 36.61/28.67. This treats Petrobras dividends received through BNDES and BNDESPar as passed through to the Treasury, an upper bound because BNDES retains part of its profit. Royalties, special participation and taxes accrue to the state whoever owns Petrobras; only the dividend share depends on ownership. In model runs Petrobras dividends follow the already-drawn C7 commodity-price path; ownership is not assumed to change production.

## Evidence qualifications

Unlisted valuations use 2021 RAEEF book equity and an assumed 0.5-1.5 price-to-book range. Payer detail ends in April 2020, so later RTN 'other' receipts cannot be assigned to individual companies and are not invented. Correios was non-dependent in RAEEF 2021: the default saving from ending Treasury-covered operating losses is zero. Postalis is shown only as a 0-R$7.799bn documented contingent-liability sensitivity, not as proceeds or a certain public cost.
