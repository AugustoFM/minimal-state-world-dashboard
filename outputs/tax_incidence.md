# Stylised tax incidence by instrument

This diagnostic applies a separate **+0.1 pp GDP rise** and **-0.1 pp GDP cut** to each instrument.
It is not a programme result or a forecast. Changes are relative to the unchanged 2025 PNAD
distribution. Raw and smoothed poverty-headcount, top-share and Gini changes are percentage points
(the Gini is multiplied by 100); poverty-gap changes are percentage points of the relevant line.
Positive poverty values mean more poverty; positive top-share or Gini values mean greater
concentration. The smoothed headcount is the mean over 21 lines from 0.95 to 1.05 times the stated
line.

Baseline PNAD raw/smoothed/FGT1 values are 3.1815%/3.1454%/1.5974% at R$218 and
23.0555%/23.6030%/9.1524% at R$759. The baseline Gini is 0.5112, with an 11.0209% top-1% income
share and a 40.2656% top-10% share. The existing 2024 IRPF table gives a top-1% share
of **25.0680% among declarants**, versus **11.0209% of person-weighted income in PNAD**. These are not
like-for-like populations, but the gap is consistent with the known PNAD understatement of top and
capital incomes; the PNAD top-income base should consequently be read as understated.

The consumption-tax rise illustrates the mass-point issue: its raw R$759 change is +1.6966 pp,
while the 21-line smoothed headcount changes by +0.1517 pp and FGT1 by +0.0498 pp of the line. The
raw value remains visible as a diagnostic; the smoothed headcount and poverty gap are the headline
R$759 measures.
