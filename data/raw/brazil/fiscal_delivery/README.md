# F1 source annexes

Each LDO (or PLDO) contains, under LRF art. 4 para 2 I, an annex evaluating compliance with the previous year's fiscal targets: the original primary-balance target, legal revisions during the year, and the outturn.

Archived (evaluated year): 2008, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2022, 2023, 2024.

Missing (to source from the Câmara / Senado LDO archives or the Tesouro RREO): 1999-2007, 2009, 2013, 2018-2021, 2025. gov.br/planejamento has no usable pages for budget years 2001-2009 and 2020-2023.

Files are untouched; names are normalised to `ldo<budget year>_avaliacao_metas_ano_anterior.<ext>`. Hashes in data/raw/SOURCES.md.
