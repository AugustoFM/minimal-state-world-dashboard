Four Years to Decide

Play out a hypothetical four-year presidency under either candidate’s own filed government programme, one year at a time. Every number comes from a real, published, open-source model of the Brazilian economy — nothing here is invented for the game.

Before you vote in the case: play this through once as Lula, once as Flávio Bolsonaro, with whatever year-by-year choices feel closest to how you’d expect each of them to actually govern. Then compare.

Companion tool to the teaching case “The Vote: Brazil’s 2026 Runoff.” Built on the same published Monte Carlo fiscal model as the case’s own Exhibits, reusing Brazil-specific sourced parameters (demography, fiscal multipliers, the Okun coefficient, household microsimulation). This is not a forecast of what either candidate will do — it plays out each candidate’s own filed programme under choices you make, not a prediction of actual governance. See the player manual for the full method and its honest limits.
